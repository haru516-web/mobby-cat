## 01-american-shorthair: アメリカンショートヘア

Silver classic tabby American Shorthair: short dense silver fur, very bold charcoal swirling bullseye flank markings, forehead M, ringed tail, green iris.

## 02-scottish-fold: スコティッシュフォールド

Cream Scottish Fold: distinctly small folded-forward downturned ears hugging round head, warm pale apricot short dense coat, amber iris.

## 03-maine-coon: メインクーン

Brown tabby Maine Coon: tall ears with lynx-tip tufts, shaggy long fur, pronounced neck ruff and square muzzle, dark brown tabby stripes, golden-green iris, large plume tail.

## 04-norwegian-forest: ノルウェージャンフォレストキャット

Norwegian Forest cat: red tabby and white, long flowing guard hairs, thick white chest ruff, tufted ears, fluffy tail, green iris.

## 05-persian: ペルシャ

White Persian: exceptionally long dense white fluffy coat, small low-set ears, gently flattened short nose and round cheeks, copper iris, huge soft plume tail.

## 06-balinese: バリニーズ

Lilac-point Balinese: ivory silky semi-long hair, pale pink-grey mask ears feet and fine plume tail, sapphire blue iris; pointed pattern clearly visible.

## 07-russian-blue: ロシアンブルー

Russian Blue: close short plush blue-grey fur with silver tipped sheen, large upright ears, emerald green iris, slender tail; avoid long shaggy coat.

## 08-abyssinian: アビシニアン

Ruddy Abyssinian: warm reddish-brown ticked short fur with dark dorsal shading, no stripes or spots on torso, large upright ears, gold iris, cream chin.

## 09-somali: ソマリ

Sorrel Somali: rich cinnamon-red ticked semi-long fur, foxlike warm red ear tufts, thick neck ruff and bushy fox tail, green iris, pale peach muzzle.

## 10-turkish-angora: ターキッシュアンゴラ

White Turkish Angora: fine silky flowing white semi-long fur, large upright ears, delicate pointed muzzle, long fine plume tail, sapphire blue iris.

## 11-turkish-van: ターキッシュバン

Red-and-white Turkish Van: almost all white semi-long fur, red patches restricted mainly to crown near ears, red ringed plume tail, amber iris.

## 12-siberian: サイベリアン

Siberian: thick plush triple-coat brown mackerel tabby, cream muzzle and chest, rounded tufted ears, broad soft muzzle, thick bushy tail, yellow-green iris.

## 13-tonkinese: トンキニーズ

Champagne mink Tonkinese: short silky warm beige fur, milk-chocolate face ears feet tail with softly blended contrast, luminous aqua turquoise iris.

## 14-exotic-shorthair: エキゾチックショートヘア

## 15-birman: バーマン

## 16-ragamuffin: ラガマフィン

## 17-american-curl: アメリカンカール

## 18-selkirk-rex: セルカークレックス

## 19-devon-rex: デボンレックス

## 20-cornish-rex: コーニッシュレックス

## 21-japanese-bobtail: ジャパニーズボブテイル

## 22-manx: マンクス

## 23-egyptian-mau: エジプシャンマウ

## 24-ocicat: オシキャット

## 25-toyger: トイガー

## 26-singapura: シンガプーラ

## 27-burmese: バーミーズ

## 28-bombay: ボンベイ

## 29-havana-brown: ハバナブラウン
