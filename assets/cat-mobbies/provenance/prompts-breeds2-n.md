## トンキニーズ（シャンパンミンク） (tonkinese-champagne-mink)

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one real cat breed Mobby, square 1254x1254 true transparent PNG with generous margin. Full body front, unified rounded-square head/body no neck, short arms and two short feet. Exactly one living eye viewer-left with round black pupil and catchlight, one black camera lens green reflective glass viewer-right, one black cross D-pad lower-left chest and two plain black circular buttons diagonal lower-right. Bengal and Ragdoll references define only Mobby silhouette, realistic fur and controls. Soft studio light. No accessories, clothing, props, text or watermark. Tonkinese champagne mink: warm buff satin short coat, softly shaded milk-chocolate points on ears, mask, paws and tail; moderate rounded wedge head, aqua iris. Keep the body distinctly lighter than the points with the soft contrast of a mink pattern.
